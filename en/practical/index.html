PRACTICAL INFORMATION

Everything for your stay

What to expect, what is included and how arrival and departure work.

The chalet

  • Detached chalet for 4 guests
  • 2 bedrooms and 1 bathroom
  • 1 double bed that can be split into 2 singles, plus 2 single beds
  • Fully equipped kitchen
  • Wi-Fi and smart TV
  • Wood-fired hot tub on the terrace
  • Enclosed garden with terrace
  • Parking at the chalet

Arrival and departure

  • Arrival from 14:00
  • Departure by 10:00
  • Minimum stay of 2 nights
  • You collect the key from a key safe

Included

  • Cleaning
  • Bed linen and towels, beds made up
  • Use of the hot tub

House rules

  • Pets are not allowed.
  • No smoking indoors. There is an ashtray outside.
  • Use the hot tub until 22:00 and cover it after use.

Booking and contact

Do you have a question about your stay? Email us at stay@betuwsebron.nl.

You can cancel up to one month before arrival.
