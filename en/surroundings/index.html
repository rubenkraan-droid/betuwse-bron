THE SURROUNDINGS · OCHTEN

The Betuwe at your own pace

Chalet 56 is at Resort Rivierendal in Ochten, on a quiet tree-lined lane. The river Waal, its floodplains and the orchards of the Betuwe are just around the corner.

On the tree-lined lane of Resort Rivierendal

The chalet stands on its own plot, with greenery at the back. The river Waal and its floodplains are close to the park. Ideal for a morning walk or a bike ride along the dike.

By car it takes about 20 minutes to the centre of Tiel, 30 minutes to Nijmegen and 35 minutes to Arnhem. The supermarket is a 5-minute drive.

THROUGH THE YEAR

A reason to come in every season

Spring

In April and May the fruit trees are in blossom. The region turns white and pink.

Summer

Long evenings on the terrace. The local fruit growers are picking cherries.

Autumn

The apple and pear harvest. Afterwards, the hot tub is waiting on the terrace.

Winter

Cold air, quiet dikes and warm water on your own terrace.

OUR TIPS

Things to do

Along the Waal dike

Cycle from Ochten along the dike to Tiel, with views over the floodplains. At Tiel a small ferry takes you across the river. The dike near Ochten is being reinforced over the coming years, so check before you set off that your route is open.

Orchards in blossom

From early April to mid-May the fruit trees are in blossom. A loop of about 50 kilometres via Kesteren and Opheusden takes you through the orchards and past old farmhouses.

Nationaal Fruitpark Ochten

A 4.5-hectare park full of fruit trees, in Ochten itself. With a barefoot trail, mini golf and a petting farm. Pleasant with children.

De Verschzaak farm shop

Fresh produce from Betuwe farmers, on the Cuneraweg in Ochten. Ideal for breakfast on the terrace.
