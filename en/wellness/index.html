Betuwse Bron / Wellness

WELLNESS · BETUWE

Your own hot tub, on the terrace

At Betuwse Bron the wellness belongs to the chalet. The hot tub stands on your own terrace and is not shared with anyone.

Just for you

No shared facilities and no time slots. You get in whenever it suits you.

The terrace opens onto an enclosed garden with a hedge all around.

Especially in the cold season

In autumn and winter this is the place to be: warm water, cold air, and then back inside. After a walk along the Waal, this is where you warm up.

The hot tub is wood-fired. You light the fire yourself and watch the water slowly warm up. So start in good time.

GOOD TO KNOW

Using the hot tub

  • Use of the hot tub is included in the price.
  • Towels are ready for you on arrival.
  • You can use the hot tub until 22:00.
  • Please cover the hot tub after use. This keeps the water clean and warm.
