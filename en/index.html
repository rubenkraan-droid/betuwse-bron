THE CHALET

Light, quiet and all yours

The bay window looks out over the garden, so you see the light change from morning to evening. The chalet stands on its own plot, with no neighbours at the terrace.

Beds are made up, towels are ready and the kitchen is fully equipped. All you need to do is arrive.

  • 2 bedrooms for 4 guests, 1 bathroom

WELLNESS

Your own source, on the terrace

After a day cycling along the dike, you light the wood-fired hot tub. No shared facilities, no time slots: the wellness belongs to the chalet and is just for you.

This is the place for autumn and winter: warm water, cold air, and then back inside.

LOCATION

Frequently asked questions

Is the wellness just for us?

Yes. The hot tub belongs to the chalet and is not shared with anyone.

How many guests does the chalet sleep?

The chalet sleeps 4. There are 2 bedrooms and 1 bathroom.

What is the minimum stay?

The minimum stay is 2 nights.

What is included in the price?

Cleaning, bed linen and use of the hot tub are included.

Where is the chalet?

At Resort Rivierendal in Ochten, in the Betuwe region of the Netherlands. The river Waal and its floodplains are close to the park.

Are pets welcome?

No. Pets are not allowed in the chalet.

How does arrival work?

You are welcome from 14:00. Departure is by 10:00. You collect the key from a key safe.

How does the hot tub work?

The hot tub is wood-fired: you heat it yourself. You can use it until 22:00. Please cover it after use.

Is smoking allowed?

Not indoors. There is an ashtray outside.

Until when can I cancel?

You can cancel up to one month before arrival.
